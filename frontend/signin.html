Sign in with a passkey

If you added a passkey in Settings → Devices, use it here — no need for the computer running Synapse.

Or pair it from the computer

  1. On the computer running Synapse, open Settings → Devices.
  2. Click Add a Device.
  3. Scan the QR code with this device's camera, or open the link it shows.

Signing in with a passkey works when Synapse is served over HTTPS and opened by its name (such as https://your-computer.local). See “Signing in with a passkey” in the README.
